Promed Agent

Your assistant inside the Promed app. Speak in Egyptian Arabic or type, and it records expenses, income, payments, sales and purchases, links each one to the right project, edits anything, exports files and answers questions. Only your owner account can see it.

promed-system.vercel.app Live now. Sign in as usual, then tap the blue sparkle button.

Start in 30 seconds

  1. Open the app and sign in. The round blue sparkle button sits in the bottom corner (on a phone, just above the tab bar). On a computer, Ctrl + J also opens it.

  2. Tap the microphone, speak, and tap it again to send. Or type. Use the paperclip to attach a photo or a PDF (a receipt, an invoice, a licence).

  3. Read the card the agent shows. Small, clear entries are already saved. Anything else waits for your Confirm.

  4. Changed your mind? Press Undo on the card, or say "الغي آخر حاجة". Undo works for 30 days.

When it saves on its own

Saves straight away

New, clear entries under 5,000 EGP: an expense, an income, a payment in or out, a sale, a purchase.

Waits for Confirm

5,000 EGP or more, any edit or delete, and anything where it had to guess (an unknown person, an unclear project).

Always undoable

Every change is logged row by row. If you edited the record afterwards, Undo warns you first.

Change the 5,000 limit on the Agent activity page. Set it to 0 to approve everything yourself.

Good to know

  • Answers take about 5 to 20 seconds; questions that need several lookups take longer.
  • Voice transcription allows about 10 voice notes per minute on the current Gemini plan. If you hit that, it switches to a backup model automatically.
  • Always glance at the card before confirming. The agent sometimes mishears a word; the card shows exactly what will be saved.
  • If a person or project is new, it says so on the card (marked new) and waits for you.
  • Your database is backed up every night at 2:30 am Cairo time, encrypted, and kept for 30 days in GitHub. The password for the backups is BACKUP_PASSPHRASE in your .env.local file. Keep a copy somewhere safe; without it the backups cannot be opened.

Recommended next

  • Supabase Pro adds point-in-time recovery, on top of the nightly backups.
  • A stronger database password. The current one is short and was shared in chat.
  • Vercel Pro. The free Hobby plan is meant for non-commercial use.
